This shows a computer model forecast (NOAA GFS, 0.25° grid, about 25 km), not buoy or station measurements. Streaks move with the forecast 10 m wind; brighter, thicker and faster means stronger. Values between grid points are interpolated, so coastline and sound detail is coarser than it looks. Tap the map to read the model wind at a point. Motion follows your device's reduced-motion setting unless you change it here; with motion off, static arrows are drawn.
