Model forecast, not measurements. Flow lines show the NOAA GFS computer forecast of 10 m wind on a 0.25° grid (cells about 25 km across), updated four times a day. Brighter, thicker and faster lines mean stronger wind.

Values between grid points are interpolated, so harbors, inlets and the sounds are coarser than they look. Near the shore the model often reads lighter than stations such as Cape Lookout (CLKN7) and Beaufort (BFTN7). Check station and buoy readings before you go.

Times are Eastern (ET). Wind data: NOAA NCEP GFS. Observed winds: NOAA NDBC, NOAA / NOS / CO-OPS, and the National Weather Service. These data were collected and made freely available by NOAA/NDBC.

Coastline: U.S. Census Bureau, 2025 cartographic boundary file, 1:500,000 (not endorsed by the Census Bureau). Shown at small scale; small creeks, marsh and inland lakes are not drawn.

General information only: this is a model forecast and can be late or inaccurate for your location. Don’t rely on it alone for safety, marine or aviation decisions; check the National Weather Service.
