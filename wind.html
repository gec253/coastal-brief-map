Model forecast, not measurements. Flow lines show the NOAA GFS computer forecast of 10 m wind on a 0.25° grid (cells about 25 km across), updated four times a day. Brighter, thicker and faster lines mean stronger wind.

Values between grid points are interpolated, so harbors, inlets and the sounds are coarser than they look. Near the shore the model often reads lighter than stations such as Cape Lookout (CLKN7) and Beaufort (BFTN7). Check station and buoy readings before you go.
